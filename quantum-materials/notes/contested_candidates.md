## Details

### 1. RuO2 altermagnetism (contested)
- Material: RuO2 (rutile), bulk crystals and epitaxial films
- Claim: RuO2 is a room-temperature collinear antiferromagnet / d-wave altermagnet with spin-split bands and a crystal (anomalous) Hall effect. Counter-claim: bulk RuO2 is nonmagnetic (muSR, neutron, ARPES); film signals are surface/interface or strain effects. In-window preprints on both sides: pro 2412.18220, 2602.15606; con/qualifying 2409.13504, 2508.15004, 2510.08064, 2511.00399, 2601.06791.
- Visibility: Smejkal et al. Science Advances 2020 (OpenAlex: 926 citations); Hiraishi et al. PRL 132, 166702 (163 citations); Kessler et al. npj Spintronics 2024 (122 citations); phys.org coverage Dec 2025.
- Notes: 2409.13504 is YYMM 2409 but its exact posting day relative to 2024-09-24 was not confirmed. 1901.00445 is the arXiv version of the Sci. Adv. 2020 paper (arXiv title 'Crystal Hall effect in Collinear Antiferromagnets').
- Sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.00445, https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.10028, https://arxiv.org/html/2409.13504v1, https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.08064, https://pith.science/paper/2508.15004, https://arxiv.org/html/2511.00399, https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.06791, https://arxiv.org/html/2602.15606, https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.18220, https://www.nature.com/articles/s44306-024-00055-y

### 2. Microsoft topological gap protocol / Majorana parity readout (contested)
- Material: InAs/Al superconductor-semiconductor heterostructure nanowires
- Claim: InAs-Al gate-defined nanowires pass the topological gap protocol (TGP) and host Majorana zero modes; interferometric single-shot parity readout and tetron X/Z loop measurements follow. Critique (Legg): TGP outcome depends on arbitrary data parameters and the readout regions are gapless/disordered. In-window: 2502.19560, 2503.08944 (comments), 2504.13240 (Microsoft response), 2507.08795 (tetron).
- Visibility: PRB 107, 245423 (OpenAlex: 216 citations); Nature 638, 651 (2025) with Microsoft 'Majorana 1' press launch; Legg's Matters Arising published in Nature 2026 with a Reply; coverage in Nature News, Science News, The Quantum Insider.
- Notes: 2504.13240 is a reply by the original authors (Aghaee et al.), listed under responses because it belongs to the Comment/Reply thread.
- Sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.02472, https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.09549, https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.19560, https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.08944, https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.13240, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.08795, https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10567-8, https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10568-7, https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-025-00683-2

## Proposed noise filter for preprint claims

Score each claim cluster (original + linked preprints); flag as NOISY if score >= 3.

1. Independent replication count = 0 (no preprint from a disjoint author set reporting the same observable) : +2
2. A 'Comment on' / 'Reply to' / 'Response to' / 'Absence of' / 'No evidence' preprint cites the original : +1 each, max +2
3. arXiv comments field contains 'withdrawn' / 'retracted', or journal version has a retraction or correction notice : +3 (withdrawn) / +1 (correction)
4. Single-sample or filamentary evidence: abstract reports onset only / no zero resistance, volume fraction < 10%, or 'sample-dependent' / 'one device' language : +1
5. No publication linkage (no DOI/journal-ref) after 12 months : +1; linked to a peer-reviewed venue : -1
6. Positive-only in-window activity from the original group only (all follow-ons share a last author) : +1

All six features are checkable from arXiv metadata (authors, comments, journal-ref, DOI), Crossref 'relation' (is-comment-on / has-reply / retraction updates), and title/abstract regexes.
