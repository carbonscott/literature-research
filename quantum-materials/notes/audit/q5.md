## 4. Headline numbers

### Claim: "non-arXiv servers other than Zenodo add only about 4% on top of arXiv in the trailing window"

Trailing-window estimates from q5.json:

| server | point | 95% range |
|---|---|---|
| OSTI | 305.3 | 212-365 |
| Research Square | 61.0 | 41-73 |
| Preprints.org | 12.8 | 8-17 |
| ChemRxiv | 5.6 | 3-8 |
| HAL | 0 | 0-7 |
| TechRxiv | 0 | 0-1 |
| **Sum** | **384.7** | **265-472** |

Against 9,878 arXiv in-scope trailing records (classified.jsonl), that is 3.9% (range 2.7-4.8%).
So "about 4%" reproduces under the method as stated. But:

- **The OSTI trailing term uses the pooled dup share (29.8%).** The trailing OSTI records' own
  dup share is 39.5%, and the censoring-corrected (2026-dated) share is 43.0%.
  - With 43.0%: OSTI T = 590 × 0.570 × 0.737 = **248 [172-296]**.
  - The total becomes **327 [225-403] = 3.3% (2.3-4.1%)**.
  - The Counts line applies the censoring correction for both windows but not for T, which is
    inconsistent.
- **About 80% of the extra is OSTI.** These are journal accepted manuscripts, not preprints. The
  actual preprint servers (Research Square, ChemRxiv, Preprints.org) add about 79 records,
  which is **0.8%**.
- **All of these are upper bounds.** Duplicates were only detected against 4 cond-mat categories
  from 2024-09-24 onward.
- **The numerator and denominator are defined differently.** The numerator comes from the hand
  audit and the denominator from the classifier scope rule. As a consistency check I used the
  classifier on both sides: non-arXiv trailing records that are in scope and have no arXiv match,
  excluding Zenodo, number 472 (4.8%). That brackets the result.
- **Including Zenodo gives 650 (6.6%).** The Headline says Zenodo adds "little except noise",
  while the table shows 266 trailing unique on-topic records for it. This needs reconciling (see
  section 3: the Zenodo on-topic label is generous).

Verdict: the number holds as a round figure. I recommend "about 3-4%", with the OSTI/preprint
split and the upper-bound caveat stated.

### Claim: "OSTI ~600 unique on-topic (censoring-corrected 603 [419-722])"

1437 × (1 − 0.4304) × 0.7368 = 603.1. The range [419.1, 721.9] comes from the Wilson interval
[0.512, 0.882], so the figure reproduces. However:

- It is an upper bound: 4-category arXiv matching only, and the 2026-dated share itself can still
  be censored.
- The range reflects only the on-topic sampling. Dup-share uncertainty is not included.
- The records are journal manuscripts (94% have a journal link).
- The OSTI harvest was truncated on 3 terms, which pushes in the other direction.

Suggested wording: "at most about 600".
