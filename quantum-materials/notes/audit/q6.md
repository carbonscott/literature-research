## 2. Headline challenge: "effectively refuted by independent null results"

### Bulk RuO2 (q6 score 4; 14 critical records, 13 "groups")

- The in-window bulk nulls are real and use many different methods:
  - neutron diffraction, arXiv:2410.05850
  - transport with RRR 152, arXiv:2412.12258
  - micro-ARPES, arXiv:2501.10649
  - quantum oscillations, arXiv:2503.20621 (PRX)
  - torque magnetometry, arXiv:2504.21138
  - Mossbauer/NFS, arXiv:2505.03250
  - resonant X-ray diffraction, arXiv:2510.13767
  - NQR on powder, arXiv:2511.00399 (the label calls this "NMR")
  - specific heat, arXiv:2512.03108
  - spin-ARPES, arXiv:2604.10659
- Caveats the wording ignores:
  - **Independence is overstated.** Four of the nulls (2501.10649, 2503.20621, 2505.07201,
    2512.03108) share Y. Maeno, S. Paul and H. Matsuki. Grouping by last author gives 13 groups;
    merging real co-author links gives about 10.
  - **Timing.** 9 of the 14 are in the *preceding* window; only 5 are trailing. The debate was
    mostly settled before the trailing year.
  - **No muSR in window.** The muSR null (arXiv:2403.10028) is pre-window. The status_note
    phrase "muSR-era follow-ups" is loose.
  - **One null is still disputed.** Lovesey's Comment arXiv:2604.10105 attacks the RXD null 2510.13767.
  - **Order survives near surfaces and under strain.** arXiv:2604.10659 finds surface
    ferrimagnetism, and arXiv:2512.04995 finds altermagnetism under strain or doping. The film
    claim is contested: 15 supportive / 11 critical.
  - **The claim's "original" is a theory paper.** arXiv:1901.00445 (Sci. Adv.) is theory. The
    experimental positive reports (neutron 2017, RXS 2019) are not in the corpus, so "refuted"
    is judged against a theory proposal.
- **[external]** A 2026 Nano Convergence review ("from conflicting experiments to emerging
  consensus") and PNAS 2026 (strained 2 nm films) both describe bulk as nonmagnetic and film
  altermagnetism as strain-stabilised.
- **Verdict:** calling bulk RuO2 "refuted" is defensible in substance but worded too strongly.
  Better: "strongly disfavoured in bulk; debate moved to strained films".

### Chiral UTe2 (q6 score 4; 4 critical, "3 groups")

- **The four critical records are not independent.**
  - J. C. S. Davis is an author on arXiv:2501.16636, 2503.17761 and 2602.02490.
  - X. Liu (a co-author of 2501.16636) is last author of 2503.17450.
  - Linking by shared authors puts all 4 into **one cluster**.
  - Two of them (2501.16636, 2503.17761) are flagged `same_group=True` because Paglione, of the
    original group, supplied the crystals.
  - arXiv:2602.02490 is a 2-author JLTP overview of the same group's earlier results.
- **None of them is a TRSB null.** All four are STM inferences of the order-parameter symmetry at
  a surface (B3u, "time-reversal conserving"). The direct TRSB null, the polar Kerr study of
  Ajeesh et al. (arXiv:2305.00589, PRX 2023), is in corpus/arxiv_supplement.jsonl but is
  **unlabelled and pre-window**.
- **In-window bulk evidence that is not labelled:**
  - arXiv:2603.11278 (Matsuda, Nat. Commun. 2026): thermal conductivity, pseudo-point-nodal gap,
    "excludes non-unitary mixing". This is critical of the claim and **independent of the STM network**.
  - arXiv:2512.13877 and osti:2562963 (Paglione, original group): point nodes, B2u (or B1u/B2u).
    This favours a single-component state but disagrees with the STM B3u.
  - osti:2479892 (Moler/Rosa, PRB 2024, preceding window): edge fields "seem to agree with
    predictions for a chiral order parameter", but no chiral domains were found, and the paper
    cites the Kerr and muSR nulls. This is the only in-window record that is quasi-supportive.
  - arXiv:2603.17905 and arXiv:2604.25896: multicomponent SC only near a tetracritical point
    (pressure or field).
- **[external]** Web sources still describe the field as divided. TRSB appears in a subset of
  samples, and an orthorhombic point group makes zero-field chirality unnatural.
- **Verdict:** "refuted by independent null results" is **not supported** for UTe2. Calibrated
  wording: "disfavoured. In-window STM (one author-linked network) and independent bulk
  thermal transport point to a single-component, time-reversal-invariant state, but they
  disagree on which one (B3u vs B2u), and the direct Kerr null is pre-window." Status label:
  "disfavoured" (or "contested"), not "refuted".

## 5. Structural observations (for the owner; not required edits)

- F1 gives +2 to *refuted* claims (0 supportive), so "noisy" merges "refuted" with
  "unreplicated". A reader of the headline may read a score of 4 as "uncertain". This could be
  split in a later iteration.
- Stance labels cover a small share of each topic neighbourhood: UTe2 has 7 of 37 in-window hits
  labelled, and it misses 2603.11278, 2512.13877, osti:2479892 and the supplement's 2305.00589.
  This supports the "floors" caveat, but it also shows that labelling can change a status, not
  only a count.
